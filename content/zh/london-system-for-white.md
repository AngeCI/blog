- d4 Bf4 e3 Nf3 Nd2 c3 Bd3
- If black pushes pawn c4, pull back your bishop to c2.
- If black goes Bd6, drop your bishop back to g3. Do not initiate the trade. If they capture your bishop, capture back with your pawn and open up the h-file.
- If black goes Bf5, you still go, but do not initiate the trade. If they capture your bishop, capture back with your queen.
- If black attacks your b-pawn, defend it with your queen.
- After all this being done, move your knight to e5.
- If your opponent captures that knight, recapture with your pawn. And then you can consider playing something like Qh5.
